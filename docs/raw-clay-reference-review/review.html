Raw clay — three focused corrections

  1. Broad, blurred marks → finer, closer circular throwing lines, with variable strength.
  2. Heavy all-over sandy grain → smoother clay with sparse fine pores and quieter mottling.
  3. Radially stretched rim texture → separate raw-surface coordinates so circular detail continues across the top. Glaze painting coordinates are unchanged.

References are visual guides only. The app maps are original deterministic procedural maps. Clay body colors, lighting, geometry, tools, Undo and firing behavior were preserved. The videos show different moisture and working stages; their gray color and exact lighting were not treated as universal clay properties.
