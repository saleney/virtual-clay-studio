Just three questions

  1. What draws your attention? Does anything distract from or get in the way of the clay?
  2. Where do you hesitate? Which control is unclear, and what did you expect it to do?
  3. What belongs together? What one grouping or layout change would help most?

A screenshot with two or three notes is plenty. Please mention your device, browser and window size. No full audit or redesign needed.

A little context, if useful

Phone controls now sit below the clay in a compact dock. Desktop settings open separately. The layout is still being explored; clay behavior, stages and saved pieces are outside this review’s scope.
