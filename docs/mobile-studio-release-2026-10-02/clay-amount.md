# Starting clay amounts — local preview

Added 5 / 10 / 15 lb starting mound choices. The existing mound remains 5 lb. Larger mounds scale in three dimensions by the cube root of their weight ratio, giving approximately twice or three times the starting material. Shaping height, radius, and slope limits scale with the selected amount, bounded by the wheel size.

Selecting a different amount starts a new mound. Reset retains the selection. Choices disappear during firing/glazing; mobile Brush hides them while the slip palette uses the same space.
