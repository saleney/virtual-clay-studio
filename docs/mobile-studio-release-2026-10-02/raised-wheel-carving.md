# Raised wheel, bottom glazes, and vertical carving — local

Wheel framing raised 25 px on mobile and 20 px on desktop. Glaze palette moved above the bottom action row on both sizes. Mobile palette and action row do not overlap; no horizontal overflow found.

Vertical carving follows successive surface hits, recording shallow local grooves in the actual clay geometry. On a rotating wheel these form a slanted/spiraling trace; on a paused wheel they follow the straight stroke. Horizontal gestures retain ring carving. Geometry and slip are both carved; changes survive firing and saving.

Browser QA exercised downward carving with the wheel turning and paused, then automatic firing-to-glazing. Both produced visible grooves and the bottom glaze palette appeared correctly. Changes remain local.
