October 11, 2026. Local app at checkpoint 1c0d42e. No source or tool changes.

The requested pieces were attempted with native pointer gestures and the studio’s keyboard controls. Actual exports and studio screenshots are saved here.

- Matcha bowl: 5 lb, Hands compression, center opening and repeated widening, Sponge cleanup, Celadon glaze, two firings. Heavy upper shoulder and uneven coverage remain.
- Moon jar: 10 lb, inward upper-side pressure, outward belly shaping, lower-side refinement, small opening, Chalk glaze, two firings. It is jar-like but not a truly spherical moon jar. The fixed base and connected profile response make a narrow rounded foot difficult.
- I ♥ NY mug: 5 lb open cup, Chalk base, paused wheel, minimum 0.55 glaze brush, repeated Cobalt letter strokes and Dusty rose heart strokes, two firings. Handle attachment is unavailable. The lettering and heart blur together; this attempt does not meet a crisp logo mug brief.

A failed solid-cup start was restarted. It remains only in the temporary session shelf. Existing saved pieces were not deleted or replaced.

Browser localStorage quota was reached. The app displayed its warning and kept the new pieces downloadable in the session gallery. Exported the actual gallery PNG data to these files, so the requested results survive closing the tab. Studio screenshots preserve the making context.

## Findings from making, rather than only heuristic review

1. Creating a wide opening takes numerous strokes; a novice may assume it has stopped responding.
2. Specific silhouettes require iterative shaping and correction. Global keyboard shaping helped with bowl height and jar width.
3. The fixed broad base and the profile redistribution complicate spherical jars and refined feet.
4. Even the minimum glaze brush is too broad/feathered for lettering at this object size. Holding still accumulates material, increasing bleed around small strokes.
5. No handle attachment or fine hard-edge decoration mode exists.
6. Browser saving can genuinely exhaust localStorage; the new warning and external downloads are necessary.

Browser automation sometimes scrolled the page when activating off-screen controls; fresh screenshots were used before further pointer work. This does not establish physical-device usability or performance. No generated raster assets, preset geometry or injected clay state were used to make these pieces.
